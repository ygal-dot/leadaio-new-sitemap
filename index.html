Questions

What is AEO?

Answer engine optimization is the work of getting your brand named, and linked as a source, inside the answers AI assistants give. Where SEO earns a blue link, AEO earns the sentence.

How is it different from SEO?

SEO works on your own site. AI answers lean on what others say about you: Reddit threads, reviews, comparison lists and publications. We work on those, and the links we earn help classic rankings too.

Which engines do you cover?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Grok. We track each one separately, because each one cites different sources.

What does a program include?

An audit of your buyer prompts, a placement plan, the placements themselves, and the monitoring dashboard with a monthly review call.

How do you work on Reddit safely?

With experienced accounts that follow each community’s rules and disclose affiliation where it is required. We answer the question first. Spam gets removed, and engines notice.

How soon will we see movement?

Placements start in the first month. Each engine refreshes on its own schedule, so we report prompt by prompt and show which source moved which answer.

Can you remove negative autocomplete suggestions?

Yes. We work on Google and Bing autocomplete for brand and founder names, and on the coverage that feeds those suggestions.

Are the logos and figures on this page real?

No. The logos, the map and the dashboard on this page are samples for demonstration. Your audit shows your real position.
