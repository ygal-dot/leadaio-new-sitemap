Questions

What is AIO?

AI optimization (AIO) is the work of getting your brand named, and cited as a source, inside the answers AI assistants give. Where SEO earns a blue link, AIO earns the sentence.

How is it different from SEO?

SEO works to rank your pages. AIO works on how AI engines understand, trust and cite your brand: your entity, the structure of your content and the citations around you. The two work well together.

Which engines do you cover?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Grok. We track each one separately, because each one answers differently.

What is the AI Visibility Score?

Your baseline: how often AI engines name your brand when buyers ask about your category, and who they name instead. See what it measures.

How do we work together?

It starts with a consultation. Then we audit, plan, launch and scale, and we review results with you in quarterly business reviews (QBRs). See how it works.

How soon will we see movement?

Work goes live in the first month. Each engine refreshes on its own schedule, so we report engine by engine and prompt by prompt.
