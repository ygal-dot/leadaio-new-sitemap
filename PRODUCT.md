## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step. Self-hosted fonts in `assets/fonts/`.

## Users

Leaders at mid-market brands: CEOs and founders, CMOs and VPs of marketing, growth and demand leaders, SEO and content leads, brand and communications, and sales leaders. Their buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews who to choose, and rivals get named in those answers instead of them.

## Product Purpose

LeadAIO is an AI visibility agency. It gets brands named and cited in AI answers through AI Optimization (AIO, GEO and AEO): entity authority, AI trust signals, structured content, citation networks, knowledge graph positioning and semantic relationship mapping. Success for a client means being named, and named first, on more of the buyer prompts that matter, measured per engine and per prompt.

## Positioning

Not a black box. The method is public on the site, and every engagement shows what moved each answer.

## Operating Context

- Six pages, and every one sells the consultation: Home, How it works, AI Visibility Score, Who we serve, About, Book a consultation.
- Entry offer: the AI Visibility Score, a baseline of how often AI engines name the brand and who they name instead.
- Engagement: consultation, then Discovery and AI audit, Strategy and architecture, Launch and optimize, Scale and lead. Results are reviewed in quarterly business reviews (QBRs), not monthly calls.
- Engines covered: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Grok.
