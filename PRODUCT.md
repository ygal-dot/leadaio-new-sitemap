## Stack

## Users

Heads of marketing, growth and SEO at B2B SaaS and tech companies. Their buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews which tool to buy before they talk to sales, and rivals get named in those answers instead of them. They evaluate LeadAIO as a managed service they would hand a program to, and they have to justify it internally.

## Product Purpose

LeadAIO is a managed AI visibility agency. It gets B2B brands named and cited in AI answers by building presence in the third-party sources the engines retrieve: Reddit and community threads, review sites, best-of lists, publications, and Google and Bing autosuggest. Success for a client means being named, and named first, on more of the buyer prompts that matter, measured per engine and per prompt.

## Positioning

AI engines rarely quote a brand's own site; they quote what others say about it. LeadAIO works on those off-site sources end to end and measures the result prompt by prompt in a monitoring dashboard, instead of selling classic on-site SEO.

## Operating Context

- Engagement starts with a 15-minute call: category, product, and the rivals AI names instead.
- Then an audit of buyer prompts (named / cited per engine), a placement plan, the placements, and a monthly review with the dashboard.
- Engines covered: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Grok.

## Product Principles

1. Show the mechanism: which source moved which answer.
2. Measure per engine and per prompt; never vague "visibility".
3. Earn placements with real expertise and disclosure; no spam, no vote games.
4. Never present sample or example data as real client results.
