Questions

What do I need to provide?

Your company name and website. If you know which rivals AI names instead of you, tell us; it helps, but it is optional.

Which engines are included?

The engines your buyers use, from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews to Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Grok.

Is the score the same as the full audit?

No. The score is your baseline. The full audit is the first stage of an engagement and goes deeper into your competitors and opportunities. See the four stages.

What happens after I get it?

We walk you through it in a consultation. If it makes sense to work together, we propose a plan. If not, you keep the score.
