How we work

Four rules every LeadAIO program follows, whatever the category.

  • Show the mechanism

    We explain what we do and what moved each answer. That is why our method is on this site.

  • Measure per engine, per prompt

    Each engine answers differently, so we track each one on the questions your buyers really ask.

  • Earn every citation

    Accurate facts, real expertise and well-structured content. Useful beats clever, and it lasts.

  • Review it together

    We run quarterly business reviews (QBRs) with your team: results, what moved them, and the next quarter’s plan.
