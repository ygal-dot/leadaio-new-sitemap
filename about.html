How we fly

Four rules every LeadAIO program follows, whatever the category.

  • Show the mechanism

    Every report says which source moved which answer. No black-box “visibility score”.

  • Measure per engine, per prompt

    Each engine cites different sources, so we track each one separately on the prompts your buyers really type.

  • Earn every placement

    Real expertise, real accounts, affiliation disclosed where communities require it. Useful answers outlast clever ones.

  • Report what is real

    Your dashboard shows your own data. Examples are labelled as examples, and we never promise a ranking.

What we will not do.

Engines and communities punish shortcuts, and so do buyers. These are off the table on every program.

  • Buy or trade Reddit votes
  • Post fake reviews or pay for five stars
  • Shill without disclosure
  • Buy links from spam networks
  • Guarantee a position in any engine
